Color Drips Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Color Drips is a small art studio: make colorful sand landscapes, swirl pools of liquid, or blow a mosaic of bubbles, and save the pictures you make. This policy describes Color Drips for Android, distributed through Google Play, and Color Drips for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro. Both keep your artwork on the device you use; each has its own section below.

On Android

The short version

Color Drips keeps your artwork on your device. There’s no Color Drips account or server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the Android app has no permission to use the internet. Color Drips never sends your artwork, or anything else, to the developer.

What Color Drips keeps on your device

Color Drips stores these in its private app storage, which other apps can’t read:

  • each artwork you make, kept editable: its sand, liquid or bubbles, their positions and colors, the vessel’s shape and turn, motion settings, and the tools you were using;
  • a small preview picture of each artwork for your gallery;
  • exact copies of any artwork file the app couldn’t read, kept rather than deleted, so a damaged save never erases your work;
  • the tools, color and vessel you used last, so a new artwork starts the way you like;
  • your appearance choice: System, Light or Dark, and your Bubbles sound preference.

Color Drips doesn’t add a lock or encryption of its own: anyone who can unlock your device and open the app can see your artwork.

Saved pictures

Save puts a picture of your artwork, a PNG image, in your device’s Pictures folder (Pictures/Color Drips). Android needs no permission for this. Once it’s there, it’s an ordinary picture on your device: your gallery app, and any app you’ve allowed to see your photos, such as a photo backup service, can see it under its own terms. Color Drips doesn’t read your other pictures.

Device tilt

When device tilt is on, Color Drips reads your device’s motion sensor while an artwork is open, to choose which way is downhill. Android needs no permission for this. The readings aren’t stored or sent anywhere; only the resulting downhill direction becomes part of your artwork. You can switch tilt off and use the direction dial instead.

Backup and moving to a new device

Android can include your artwork and your Color Drips preferences in your device backup, and can copy them to a new device when you set it up. Color Drips allows cloud backup only when Android encrypts it end to end with your screen lock (PIN, pattern or password), so the developer can’t read it. Without that encryption, Color Drips is left out of cloud backup. A direct transfer to a new device goes from device to device rather than through a server. Saved pictures in your Pictures folder follow your gallery’s or photo app’s own backup settings.

You control backup in your device’s settings. The Android app doesn’t sync with other devices.

What Color Drips doesn’t do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.
  • No internet access: the Android app doesn’t request the permission.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files, and it doesn’t read your photos.

Google Play and Android may give the developer statistics such as install counts, and crash or freeze reports from people who chose to share diagnostics with Google. Google provides those reports under its own privacy policy. Color Drips doesn’t write your artwork into logs or diagnostics.

Keeping and deleting your artwork

Your artwork stays on your device until you remove it. Starting something new keeps the previous artwork in your gallery. Color Drips doesn’t yet have a way to delete a single artwork inside the app; to remove everything Color Drips keeps on a device, clear its storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Pictures you saved stay in your Pictures folder until you delete them there. An encrypted backup in your Google account follows your Google backup settings.

Color Drips never sends your artwork to the developer, so there’s nothing for the developer to delete on a server.

On iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro

The short version

On Apple devices, Color Drips keeps the same editable studio library on the device you use. It doesn’t sync between devices, and there is no Color Drips account, server, advertising, analytics or tracking. Color Drips doesn’t send your artwork to the developer.

What Color Drips keeps on your device

Color Drips keeps your editable artwork, gallery previews, exact copies of any artwork file it couldn’t read, your last-used tools, color and vessel, and your appearance and Bubbles sound preferences in its private app storage. On devices that save pictures to Photos, local export records help recognize artwork already saved there and avoid accidental duplicate pictures. These records stay on your device.

Saved pictures

On iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro, Save adds a picture of your artwork to Photos. The first time, your device asks whether Color Drips may add photos; that permission lets it add pictures but not see your library. On Mac, Save asks where to put the picture, and Color Drips can write only the file you choose: it runs in Apple’s app sandbox with access only to files you pick.

Device tilt

On iPhone and iPad, Color Drips can read your device’s motion sensors while an artwork is open, to choose which way is downhill. The readings aren’t stored or sent anywhere; only the resulting direction becomes part of your artwork.

Backups

Your device’s own backups, such as iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer, may include your Color Drips library, under Apple’s settings and terms. Pictures you saved to Photos follow your Photos settings.

Deleting your artwork

To remove everything Color Drips keeps on an Apple device, delete the app. Pictures you saved stay in Photos, or wherever you saved them on your Mac, until you delete them there.
