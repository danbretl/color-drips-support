Twelve ways to create.

Choose an experience, add a little color, and see what happens.

Sand

Pour, rake and rearrange colored grains.

Liquid

Drop color into pools, then stir and swirl.

Bubbles

Blow, spray, nudge and pop a colorful mosaic.

Clouds

Build billowing puffs and sculpt a little sky.

Fire

Shape flickering flames, embers and candlelight.

Light

Draw glowing pools and sweeping beams.

Plants

Grow moss and ferns, then sway and trim.

Magnets

Scatter colored metal, then attract and repel.

Fish

Paint swimming fish and guide their paths.

Butterflies

Arrange patterned wings, then let them flutter.

Flowers

Grow flowers, shape their petals and revive them.

Eyes

Make blinking eyes that follow your touch.

Creation illustrations from the app. Each experience gives you its own tools to make something of your own.

A collection that’s yours.

Make it your way.

Choose your colors and canvas shape. Let your creation move, or bring it to rest while you arrange it.

Come back to it.

Your work saves automatically in an editable gallery on your device. Open a creation and pick up where you left off.

Keep a little moment.

Save a picture, or record 3, 5 or 10 seconds of your moving canvas as a silent video.

Questions about the app

Your art stays with you.

No account, ads, analytics or tracking. Your editable collection stays on the device where you make it; it doesn’t sync between devices.

Read the privacy policy

Questions?

Ask in public GitHub issues. You’ll need a GitHub account. Posts are public, so share only what you’re happy for anyone to see.
