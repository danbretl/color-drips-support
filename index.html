Color you can play with.

Create with Sand, Liquid, Bubbles, Clouds, Fire, Light, Plants, Magnets, Fish, Butterflies, Flowers and Eyes. Return to your editable creations, or save a picture or short, silent canvas video.

Pour and rake sand, stir liquid, pop bubbles, sculpt clouds, or paint with living creatures. Turn the vessel, choose your colors, and let each experience move or come to rest as you play.

Everything you make stays editable in your gallery, ready to continue, and stays on your device. There’s no Odd Little Art account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the Android app has no internet access. The privacy policy explains exactly what the app keeps and where.

Questions

Ask in public GitHub issues. Posting requires a GitHub account, and everything you post there is public, so share only what you’re happy for anyone to see.
