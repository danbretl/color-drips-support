Color you can play with.

Make colorful sand landscapes, swirl pools of liquid, or blow a mosaic of bubbles. Return to your editable creations, or save a picture.

Pour and rake sand, drop and stir liquid, or blow, nudge and pop bubbles. Turn the vessel, choose your colors, and let each material move or come to rest. Every grain, pool and bubble keeps its color as you play.

Everything you make stays editable in your gallery, ready to continue, and stays on your device. There’s no Color Drips account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the Android app has no internet access. The privacy policy explains exactly what the app keeps and where.

Questions
