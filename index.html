Pour, drop, stir, tilt.

Color Drips is a small art studio. Pour colored sand into shaped glass, or drop and stir pools of colored liquid, then save the picture.

Sand falls and piles the way grains do. Stage holds it still while you place it; Gravity lets it go. Turn the vessel, or tilt your device, and every grain keeps its color as it tumbles to the new downhill. Liquid pools keep their colors too: stir them into ribbons, swirl them, and let them flow.

Everything you make stays editable in your gallery, ready to continue, and stays on your device. There’s no Color Drips account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the Android app has no internet access. The privacy policy explains exactly what the app keeps and where.

Questions
